# WORK-03: Workflow - Artifact Model Rendering

## 1. Overview
The Artifact Model is a **Compressor and Surface Renderer**, not a Discovery Engine. It renders a fixed logical artifact payload using only governed MARA exhaust / the frozen query-resolved state.

It may select geometry, compress presentation, reorder prominence, and render. It may not rediscover candidate truth or alter provenance to make a design fit.

## 2. Operational Steps

### Step 1: Receipt of Governed MARA Payload
- **Action:** accept the frozen query-resolved state / Derivative Evidence Pack and canonical logical artifact representation.
- **Verification:** confirm provenance, semantic authority, chronology state, and any `USER_OVERRIDE` event/receipt metadata are intact.
- **Restriction:** the Renderer is forbidden from using raw candidate corpus or raw target material to create new candidate truth.

### Step 2: Selection of Pertinent Evidence
- Use only admitted, bound, and authorized candidate propositions.
- Do not include SCOUT Exhaust as candidate evidence.
- Do not convert MARA non-binds into negative public résumé claims.
- Preserve the strongest semantically authorized relationship; do not inflate agency, ownership, causation, scope, or competency.

### Step 3: Freeze the Logical Artifact Payload
Before visual geometry selection:

- finalize the pre-render logical artifact representation;
- calculate character volume and countable-line volume;
- preserve section distribution, chronology depth, project depth, education footprint, and wrapping-sensitive strings;
- freeze User Override provenance accounting.

No subsequent surface decision may modify candidate truth merely to fit geometry.

### Step 4: Deterministic Résumé Surface Selection
For résumé artifacts, select from the retained surface registry defined in:

`architecture/MARA_RESUME_SURFACE_REGISTRY_2026-08-18.md`

and represented by:

`schemas/SCHEMA_RESUME_SURFACE_REGISTRY.md`.

Selection sequence:

1. classify payload density;
2. reject surfaces below comfortable character capacity;
3. reject surfaces whose container topology conflicts with payload shape;
4. apply PDF-fitness, wrap-slack, and typography constraints;
5. apply destination presentation requirements;
6. rank remaining valid surfaces by fit;
7. select deterministically when one surface is materially superior;
8. when multiple surfaces remain materially equivalent, permit a user surface choice among those already-valid surfaces.

A surface choice is an Artifact Model design decision. It does not alter MARA evidence or `USER_OVERRIDE` provenance.

### Step 5: Compression & Synthesis
- Render the bound evidence into the required format.
- Every meaningful assertion must remain within the semantic authority established by MARA.
- Do not bridge evidence or chronology gaps.
- A known declared gap may inform visual attention strategy without forcing disclosure or implying invented continuity.

### Step 6: Traceability Audit
Map every rendered assertion back through:

`Rendered Assertion → Logical Artifact Proposition → MARA Bound Coordinate / Evidence Pack → Evidence Atom / USER_OVERRIDE event when applicable → Source/Authority`

If a rendered assertion cannot be traced to its governing authority path, delete or correct it.

### Step 7: Geometry Validation
Validate the actual rendered surface against the fixed payload:

- no semantic content lost because of container pressure;
- no font/margin degradation below validated surface floors;
- no destructive title/organization wrapping;
- no overlap/collision;
- correct chronology ordering and distinct overlapping accountability tracks;
- subordinate/deep content remains subordinate when required;
- PDF/HTML behavior does not alter provenance accounting.

### Step 8: Artifact Emission
Generate the final target-resolved artifact and carry the provenance/performance receipt as inspectable metadata or linked audit state.

## 3. User Override Handling

The Artifact Model may consume `USER_OVERRIDE` information as operational authority for the affected artifact requirement after MARA has recorded the event.

It must not:

- treat the override as independent evidence;
- increase upstream authority;
- hide override provenance;
- change override percentages based on wrapping or visual geometry.

All override accounting remains governed by the canonical pre-render representation.

## 4. Supported Artifact Types

- Target-Resolved Résumé
- Recruiter Summary
- Interview Prep Brief
- Cover Letter / Integrated Package
- HTML résumé / email-front-door pair where separately governed by delivery constraints

## 5. Prohibited Behaviors

- **NO Discovery:** do not infer or discover candidate skills outside the governed MARA payload.
- **NO Hallucination:** every claim requires a governing evidence/authority path.
- **NO Identity Rewriting:** artifact projection does not modify candidate identity.
- **NO Surface Rescue:** never rewrite candidate truth to save an over-capacity or poorly matched surface.
- **NO Provenance Reflow:** PDF/HTML geometry cannot change User Override attribution.

## 6. Exit Criteria

- logical payload remains unchanged in meaning;
- selected surface is valid for payload capacity and topology;
- traceability contract satisfied;
- semantic authority preserved;
- chronology/accountability state preserved;
- any User Override receipt remains intact;
- final artifact renders without geometry failure.
