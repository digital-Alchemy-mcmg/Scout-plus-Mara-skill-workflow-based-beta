# WORK-02: Workflow - MARA Binding & Projection

## 1. Overview
MARA is a read-only interrogation engine against admitted candidate truth. It does not search for keywords; it tests the Bind between structured job demands and frozen candidate evidence. Candidate-state construction and chronology accountability occur before destination projection.

## 2. Pre-Freeze Chronology Accountability

Before Candidate Freeze, MARA must attempt the governing six-year chronology-accountability pass.

- Work History is the primary chronology-bearing domain.
- Legitimate temporal anchors may come from other admitted Spatial Candidate DNA domains.
- Declared gaps are valid chronological states.
- A temporal anchor may occupy only the span supported by its evidence.
- Unknown material gaps may remain unresolved; MARA may not invent activity to close them.

Governing specification: `architecture/MARA_SIX_YEAR_CHRONOLOGY_ACCOUNTABILITY_2026-08-18.md`.

## 3. Operational Steps

### Step 1: Query Insertion (Stage 4)
- **Action:** Accept the `INTF-01` Query Bundle from SCOUT.
- **Receptor Setup:**
  - Attach receptors to the Candidate Core / immutable candidate state.
  - Activate the query-resolved semantic walls required by the bundle.
  - Preserve evidence-class authority and provenance boundaries.

### Step 2: Traversal (Stage 5)
- **Action:** Traverse frozen Candidate DNA against each Demand Primitive.
- **Evaluation:** determine whether the frozen record supports, contradicts, partially binds, or does not bind the demand.
- **Absence:** no supporting evidence is `unsupported`; absence is not negative evidence.

### Step 3: Corroboration & Counterposition
- **Convergent Corroboration:** identify genuinely independent evidence directions supporting the same candidate coordinate.
- **Recurrence:** repetition may establish recurrence/legitimacy but does not become independent corroboration without source independence.
- **Counterposition:** ensure governing verbs preserve the evidenced relationship and do not inflate agency, ownership, causation, scope, or competency.

### Step 4: Exhaust Formation (Stage 6)
- **Action:** collect legitimate target demands that failed to bind.
- **Categorization:** preserve reasons such as `unsupported`, `contradicted`, or `insufficient_authority` without converting non-binds into negative candidate evidence.
- **Output:** MARA Exhaust Map.

### Step 5: Freeze & Snapshot
- **Action:** candidate evidence admission is already closed before destination traversal; create the query-resolved frozen projection state / Derivative Evidence Pack for the target.
- **Constraint:** downstream stages may select, bind, rank, contextualize, and project admitted evidence but may not manufacture candidate truth.

## 4. Mandatory Artifact Requirement Halt

If downstream construction exposes a **mandatory artifact requirement** that cannot be legally resolved from admitted evidence:

1. MARA halts at the evidentiary boundary.
2. The unresolved requirement and halt state are recorded.
3. MARA must not infer harder merely because the artifact is mandatory.
4. The user may supply/promote resolution under `USER_OVERRIDE`.
5. `USER_OVERRIDE` is operational authority, not evidentiary authority.
6. Override-dependent downstream construction must remain traceable to the override event.

Governing specification: `architecture/MARA_USER_OVERRIDE_PROVENANCE_PERFORMANCE_2026-08-18.md`.
Schema: `schemas/SCHEMA_USER_OVERRIDE.md`.

## 5. User Override Measurement

All override measurement occurs on the canonical pre-render artifact representation.

- `H` = countable lines at halt when legitimately available.
- literal user-supplied lines are counted separately.
- propagation delta measures downstream artifact growth causally unlocked by the override.
- multiple override propagation intervals may not overlap.
- unavailable telemetry remains unavailable and must never be fabricated.

Rendered PDF/HTML wrapping, typography, pagination, or surface geometry never changes override attribution.

## 6. Evaluative Boundaries

- **Ceiling Check:** strongest authorized positive support remains bounded by evidence class.
- **Floor Check:** only actual adverse/contradictory evidence occupies Floor; missing evidence does not.
- **Semantic Check:** rendered language may not exceed the evidence-supported relationship.
- **Chronology Check:** declared gaps preserve accountability; unknown mandatory chronology may halt rather than be inferred.

## 7. Handoff Criteria

MARA may hand off to the Artifact Model only when:

- frozen candidate/projection state is valid;
- Derivative Evidence Pack or equivalent governed exhaust is available;
- mandatory unresolved requirements are either legitimately absent from the artifact or resolved through a recorded `USER_OVERRIDE`;
- provenance boundaries are intact;
- any override receipt fields are carried downstream without reinterpretation.
