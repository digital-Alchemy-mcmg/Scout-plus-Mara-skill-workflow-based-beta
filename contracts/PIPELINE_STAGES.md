# CONT-01: Pipeline Stages (The Seven Gates)

This document defines the contractual sequence of operations. Every execution must pass these gates in order.

## 1. Pipeline Overview
The system moves matter through seven stages, transitioning responsibility between the three machines (SCOUT → MARA → Artifact Model).

| Stage | Name | Machine | Primary Operation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Intake & Filtration** | SCOUT | Separate pertinent target data from noise. |
| **2** | **Decomposition** | SCOUT | Atomic breakdown into Demand Primitives. |
| **3** | **Classification** | SCOUT | NAICS/O*NET comprehension diagnostics. |
| **4** | **Query Insertion** | MARA | Formulate demand as query/receptor structure against frozen candidate state. |
| **5** | **Binding** | MARA | Traverse Candidate DNA vs. demand while preserving evidence authority and provenance. |
| **6** | **Exhaust / Governed Handoff Formation** | MARA | Preserve non-binds, contradictions, Derivative Evidence Pack, and any mandatory-requirement halt state. |
| **7** | **Projection & Rendering** | ARTIFACT | Freeze logical payload, select a valid surface, render, and validate traceability. |

## 2. Candidate-State Preconditions

Before destination traversal, MARA constructs candidate truth independently of the target and closes normal evidence admission at Candidate Freeze.

Work History must attempt the governing six-year chronology-accountability pass before freeze. Declared gaps are valid chronology states; unknown material gaps remain unresolved rather than being filled by inference.

## 3. Stage Details & Gates

### Stage 1: Intake & Filtration
- **Action:** apply the Admission Rule to the target source.
- **Exit Gate:** admitted material has functional/operational consequence.
- **Exhaust:** SCOUT Exhaust contains discarded target noise and is never candidate evidence.

### Stage 2: Decomposition
- **Action:** break admitted target text into atomic Demand Primitives.
- **Exit Gate:** explicit operational demands are represented without omission-driven reinterpretation.

### Stage 3: Classification
- **Action:** resolve NAICS and O*NET/SOC as comprehension diagnostics.
- **Exit Gate:** classification is explicit enough to test whether SCOUT understood the economic/occupational neighborhood.

### Stage 4: Query Insertion
- **Action:** SCOUT hands target primitives to MARA; MARA forms the destination-side query against frozen Spatial Candidate DNA.
- **Exit Gate:** destination demand has not altered candidate truth.

### Stage 5: Binding
- **Action:** traverse frozen Candidate DNA.
- **Exit Gate:** each demand receives a governed bind/non-bind state with provenance and semantic authority preserved.

### Stage 6: Exhaust / Governed Handoff Formation
- **Action:** preserve legitimate target demands that did not bind and produce the governed MARA handoff / Derivative Evidence Pack.
- **Exit Gate:** non-binds are categorized without turning absence into negative evidence.

If a **mandatory artifact requirement** remains legally unresolved from admitted evidence, MARA halts at the evidentiary boundary. The user may resolve that requirement through a recorded `USER_OVERRIDE` event.

`USER_OVERRIDE` is operational authority, not evidentiary authority. It must remain traceable and measured on the canonical pre-render artifact representation.

Governing specification:

`architecture/MARA_USER_OVERRIDE_PROVENANCE_PERFORMANCE_2026-08-18.md`

### Stage 7: Projection & Rendering
- **Action:** accept only governed MARA exhaust; construct and freeze the logical pre-render artifact payload.
- **Surface Gate:** select a résumé surface from the retained registry using payload volume, density, topology, PDF fitness, wrap slack, and destination constraints.
- **User Surface Choice:** if multiple already-valid surfaces remain equivalent, the user may choose among them; that choice cannot alter candidate truth or provenance.
- **Render Gate:** every meaningful rendered assertion must trace to admitted evidence or an explicit operational authority event such as `USER_OVERRIDE`.

Governing surface specification:

`architecture/MARA_RESUME_SURFACE_REGISTRY_2026-08-18.md`

## 4. Dual Exhaust Rule

The pipeline produces two distinct classes of residue that must never be conflated:

1. **SCOUT Exhaust:** irrelevant target-source material rejected before demand construction.
2. **MARA Exhaust:** legitimate target demand that did not bind to the frozen candidate record.

A MARA non-bind is not automatically adverse evidence and does not create a Floor coordinate.

## 5. Geometry / Provenance Separation

Artifact surface geometry is downstream of candidate truth and override accounting.

PDF wrapping, HTML responsive wrapping, typography, pagination, container width, or surface selection may change visual presentation but may not change:

- candidate evidence;
- evidence authority;
- chronology truth;
- `USER_OVERRIDE` attribution;
- canonical pre-render line accounting.
