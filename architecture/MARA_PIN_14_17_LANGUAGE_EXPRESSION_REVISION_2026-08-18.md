# MARA PIN-14 THROUGH PIN-17 — LANGUAGE EXPRESSION REVISION

Status: Pilot-derived production revision
Source state: Governed Artifact-Input Freeze already established; UO-001 chronology/accountability state remains closed.
Scope: PIN-14 through PIN-17 only.

## Coupled subsystem invariant
PIN-14 determines what the frozen relationship may safely mean in expression. PIN-15 determines which verb/relationship family may safely say it. PIN-16 removes machine/governance language and normalizes the authorized proposition into ordinary human professional prose. PIN-17 emits the human-ready blurb that PIN-18 measures.

No pin in this subsystem may create new evidence, increase authority, repair chronology, infer unsupported agency, invent metrics, merge distinct coordinates, or use layout pressure as justification for semantic change.

## PIN-14 — Semantic-Safe Neighborhood Resolution v2
Input: Cleared Derived Evidence Pack + frozen artifact-input state.
Operation: For each selected frozen proposition, identify a bounded neighborhood of human expressions that preserve agency, object, scope, temporal state, evidence authority, limitations, and semantic ceiling. The neighborhood is an allowed meaning-space, not a synonym list.
Output: Semantic Expression Envelope for every proposition admitted to human expression.

Controls: preserve candidate agency, object/scope, chronology where expressed, parallel-coordinate distinctions, and material project evolution; prohibit unsupported ownership, causation, outcomes, scale, metrics, credentials, or role elevation; prevent internal governance terminology from becoming résumé prose.

## PIN-15 — Verb-Family Resolution v2
Input: Semantic Expression Envelopes.
Operation: Select the strongest natural verb family that remains inside the envelope. Example families: managed/supervised/handled; participated/worked with/supported; developed/designed where established; implemented where established; used/worked with for tool use; evolved/expanded/shifted for state change.
Output: Authorized Verb / Relationship Family.

Prohibited escalation includes used→engineered, participated→led, worked with→designed, responsibility→executive ownership, and chronology/accountability→substantive verification.

## PIN-16 — Machine-to-Human Prose Normalization v2
Input: Frozen proposition + Semantic Expression Envelope + Authorized Verb Family.
Operation: Rewrite into ordinary professional language while preserving frozen meaning. Remove system-facing qualification syntax, provenance prose, ontology labels, validation language, internal state labels, and downstream instructions. Keep the constraint operationally underneath the prose rather than printing it into the résumé.
Output: Human-Normalized Evidence Units.

Critical rule: removing machine language does not remove the machine constraint.

## PIN-17 — Human-Ready Blurb Generation v2
Input: Human-Normalized Evidence Units.
Operation: Emit actual résumé-ready textual units understandable without MARA context, natural to a hiring reader, traceable to frozen propositions, and ready for PIN-18 measurement without further semantic rewriting. Closely related units may combine only when combination does not merge independent coordinates, hide contradiction/limitation, create stronger agency, or fabricate causality.
Output: Human-Ready Blurb Set.

## PIN-18 handoff
PIN-18 measures the human-ready renderable payload emitted by PIN-17, not earlier governance prose. It may count and classify but may not rewrite blurbs to improve capacity. Required telemetry includes total characters, non-whitespace characters, words, blurbs, sections, per-section volume, longest/wrapping-sensitive strings, employment/accountability/education entry counts, subordinate-content volume, wrap pressure, and Surface Registry capacity inputs.

## Handoff invariant
PIN-14 bounds meaning. PIN-15 bounds verbs. PIN-16 humanizes authorized meaning. PIN-17 emits measurable human text. PIN-18 measures exactly what PIN-17 emits.
