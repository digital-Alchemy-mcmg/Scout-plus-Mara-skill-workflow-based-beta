# MARA Six-Year Chronology Accountability Addendum

**Status:** GOVERNING — admitted at 2026-08-18 synchronization  
**System:** MARA Spatial Candidate DNA  
**Primary Domain:** Work History  
**Cross-Domain Scope:** any admitted candidate evidence carrying legitimate temporal anchors  
**Purpose:** reduce avoidable chronology traversal failures and User Override events before Candidate Freeze.

## 1. Governing Requirement

Before Candidate Freeze, MARA must attempt to establish a **six-year timeline of accountability** for the candidate, measured backward from the relevant artifact date.

The requirement is not six years of conventional employment. It is six years of **candidate temporal accountability where the evidence permits it**.

Work History remains the primary chronology-bearing domain, but legitimate temporal anchors may come from Work History, Education & Technical Competency, Creative Works & Projects, References / Publications / Testimony / Observed & Inferred Behavior, Candidate Core where a legitimate biographical temporal anchor exists, and other already-admitted candidate evidence carrying supported dates or duration.

Cross-domain chronology may connect supported temporal points. It may not fabricate activity between them.

## 2. Why This Requirement Exists

Chronology failures create lineage gaps. Lineage gaps can destroy traversal vectors. When MARA cannot determine how candidate evidence relates across time, it may be unable to construct a mandatory artifact chronology without inference.

The preferred defense is upstream:

**better chronological evidence capture → stronger six-year accountability → fewer lineage gaps → stronger traversal continuity → fewer chronology halts → fewer User Overrides**

MARA should improve temporal accountability before freeze rather than infer harder after freeze.

## 3. Valid Accountability States

A material interval may be accounted for by supported periods of employment, education, formal training, independent work, contract or freelance activity, substantial projects, entrepreneurship/company formation, documented professional development, explicitly declared self-directed learning, caregiving/personal leave/sabbatical/unemployment, another candidate-owned activity carrying legitimate temporal support, or an explicitly declared gap.

Chronology accountability is broader than résumé-worthy content.

## 4. Declared Gaps Are Valid Chronological States

A declared gap is a valid chronological state.

The six-year accountability requirement does not require every interval to be occupied by employment, education, projects, training, or another résumé-bearing activity. It requires that material time be **accounted for**.

**Unknown time is a chronology problem.**  
**Declared time is not.**

A declared gap closes the chronology break because MARA now knows the interval is intentionally unoccupied or otherwise not represented by résumé-bearing activity.

MARA must not attempt to fill a declared gap merely because it contains no conventional work history.

A declared gap is sufficient to preserve traversal continuity.

## 5. Environmental Constraint Against Inventing Time

Every chronology anchor carries only the temporal span supported by its evidence.

An isolated dated event does not imply continuous occupation of the surrounding period.

Example: if employment ends in 2024 and resumes in 2027, a project created in 2025 does not resolve the entire 2024–2027 gap. If evidence supports six to seven months of project activity, MARA may account for approximately that interval and no more. If separate admitted evidence establishes six to eight months of company-formation or SBA activity, that is a separate anchor. The anchors may collectively reduce uncertainty, but neither may be stretched beyond its supported duration.

MARA must not extend a short project across a larger gap, infer continuous employment from a dated accomplishment, convert a point-in-time event into an occupied period, merge adjacent activities into continuous occupation without support, or infer what occurred before or after a dated activity merely because a larger gap exists.

Approximate duration remains approximate. A point remains a point. A gap remains a gap unless supported evidence or explicit candidate declaration resolves it.

## 6. Chronology Sufficiency

A six-year timeline is sufficiently accountable when MARA possesses enough supported temporal states to traverse the relevant period without a mandatory chronology failure.

This does not require an event in every month.

A material interval may be represented by supported work activity, supported education/training/project/entrepreneurial activity, a supported point-in-time anchor when only a point is known, or an explicitly declared gap.

The governing target is:

**continuous accountability of time, not continuous occupation.**

## 7. Relationship to Work History

Work History must optimize for chronology as part of its upstream responsibility, but chronology is broader than employment history.

If the complete Spatial Candidate DNA already establishes sufficient chronology through legitimate cross-domain anchors, Work History must not manufacture additional records merely to satisfy a quota.

## 8. Relationship to Candidate Freeze

The six-year accountability pass must be attempted before Candidate Freeze.

If chronology remains materially ambiguous after legitimate cross-domain temporal traversal, the ambiguity survives freeze. Freeze does not authorize later inference.

If the unresolved chronology becomes mandatory for an artifact, MARA may legitimately halt and invoke the normal `USER_OVERRIDE` mechanism.

## 9. Relationship to Artifact Presentation

The chronology layer and artifact projection layer are separate.

A period may be sufficient for internal temporal accountability while remaining inappropriate for external résumé presentation.

If MARA knows a gap is real and declared, the Artifact Model may treat it as a known constraint and manage attention without forcing disclosure. It may emphasize relevant duties, accomplishments, or a better-matching prior role while preserving correct ordering.

The renderer may not invent activity, imply unsupported continuity, or erase the internal chronology state.

**known gap → no invention → no forced disclosure → deliberate attention management**

## 10. Governing Constraints

1. Target six years of timeline accountability, not mandatory employment.
2. Work History is primary chronology-bearing domain; legitimate cross-domain anchors are allowed.
3. Cross-domain chronology may connect supported points but not fabricate activity between them.
4. Missing periods remain missing when evidence does not support resolution.
5. Absence of evidence is not negative evidence.
6. Declared gaps are valid chronological states.
7. The six-year accountability attempt occurs before Candidate Freeze.
8. Chronology already established across Spatial Candidate DNA is not redundantly reconstructed inside Work History.
9. Temporal spans may not exceed their evidence.
10. A point-in-time event may not be stretched into an occupied period.
11. A known gap may inform presentation strategy without forcing disclosure.
12. Any unresolved mandatory chronology after freeze remains eligible for the normal User Override halt-and-handoff process.

## 11. Governing Principle

> **MARA must account for time without inventing what occupied it.**

A known empty interval is preferable to an invented occupied interval. A declared gap preserves lineage. An unknown material gap may break traversal.
